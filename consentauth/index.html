Original research

Neural data regulation standards

Neural signals can reveal cognition and emotion. Because of the mental privacy risks, laws and bills on neural data have spread in the US and abroad.

ConsentAuth’s consent model was built with these rules in mind, and covers other highly sensitive data such as Part 2 substance use records and reproductive health data.
