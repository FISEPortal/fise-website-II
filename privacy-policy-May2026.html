19. ConsentAuth and Health Information

19.1 Whose Data

Healthcare organizations and health apps (our "customers") use ConsentAuth to record the permissions behind sharing their patients' and users' data. For those records, we act on the customer's behalf and under our agreement with the customer. The customer's own privacy notice, or for HIPAA covered entities its Notice of Privacy Practices, explains how the customer uses and shares its patients' and users' data. If a patient or user asks us about their records, we forward the request to the customer.

19.2 What ConsentAuth Stores

  • A pseudonymous ID the customer chooses for each patient or user. It is never a name, email address, or phone number.
  • Permission records: the wording and version shown, each approval, decline, and revocation, and when it happened.
  • For HIPAA authorizations: that the authorization was signed, who signed it (the patient or user, or a personal representative and the authority they stated), how the customer identified the signer, the date, and the expiration. The patient's or user's name appears on the consent screen and on their copy, but it is not sent to or stored by ConsentAuth.
  • Records of permission checks and data shares (which partner, which purpose, and when), notices sent to partners, and deletion requests and their confirmations.
  • Agreements that customers and partners upload, such as business associate agreements and data sharing agreements.
  • Records of de-identification done with the ConsentAuth tool. The tool runs on the customer's own computer or in their browser, so the data being de-identified does not reach ConsentAuth; only a record of what was done does.
  • Accounts for customer and partner staff: name, work email address, and sign-in records.

ConsentAuth does not store clinical or health data itself, such as lab results, visit notes, or wearable data.

19.3 Health Information and Business Associate Agreements

Permission records about a patient can be protected health information under HIPAA. FISE signs a business associate agreement (BAA) with healthcare customers that are HIPAA covered entities or business associates, and handles those records as the BAA requires. For consumer health apps, we handle permission records as our agreement with the customer requires.

19.4 Security and Retention

ConsentAuth records are encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to the customer's authorized staff, and to partners for the notices sent to them. Permission records are kept on tamper-evident logs and retained as the customer agreement and applicable law require.
